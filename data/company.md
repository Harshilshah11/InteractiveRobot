## Technology and autonomy stack
also: what technology do you use, technology, how do your robots work, autonomy stack, software, ground control station, GCS, autonomy engine, how is the robot controlled, architecture, AI
Arnobot's robots run on a four-layer autonomy stack: a ground control station, an autonomy engine, real-time control and the robot hardware.
The ground control station is the human layer for mission planning and monitoring, and the autonomy engine runs on the robot, fuses the sensors, holds the map and decides the next move.
The real-time control layer reacts thousands of times a second, deterministically, while the hardware layer changes from platform to platform.

## NEXUS operating conditions
also: nexus temperature, what terrain for nexus, nexus range, where can nexus operate, nexus limits
NEXUS operates between 0 and 45 degrees Celsius on indoor surfaces, rough ground and moderate slopes.
Its control range is 100 to 300 metres.
Avoid deep water and areas with strong electromagnetic interference.

## NEXUS safety guidelines
also: nexus safety, nexus precautions, nexus warnings, is nexus waterproof, nexus heat
Avoid water immersion unless the unit is waterproof-rated, and keep NEXUS away from extreme heat above 50 degrees Celsius.
Use only the approved battery and charger, and never disassemble the electronics while powered.
Do not leave the unit in direct sunlight for long periods or inside closed hot vehicles, and do not force movement manually when it is powered off.

## NEXUS charging
also: how do I charge nexus, nexus charger, charging time, charging current
Turn the robot off, connect the charger to the charging port and plug it into a power source, then connect the four-pin aviation plug to the balance charger.
Charge at a 2.2 amp current setting, with a typical charging time of one to two hours.

## NEXUS controls
also: how do I drive nexus, nexus joystick, nexus remote setup, nexus buttons
Insert batteries into the remote, turn on the remote, then turn on the robot and confirm the connection via the indicator light.
The joystick moves NEXUS forward and backward when pushed up and down, and left and right when pushed sideways.
Switch A toggles the light and switch B toggles the mine trigger.

## NEXUS maintenance
also: how do I look after nexus, nexus cleaning, nexus care, nexus storage
Clean the wheels after use, check screws and joints regularly, and keep the camera lens clean.
Recharge the battery after each mission and store the unit in a dry, cool place.

## NEXUS troubleshooting
also: nexus not working, nexus wont turn on, no video feed, remote not connecting, wheels not moving, nexus problem
If NEXUS does not turn on, the battery is likely empty, so recharge it.
If the remote will not connect, re-pair the remote.
If the wheels do not move, tighten the screws on a loose assembly, and if there is no video feed, restart the system.

also: what is in the box, what do I get with saibya, delivery contents, accessories
A Saibya delivery includes the UGV, a Jumper T14 transmitter, a battery, panel lock keys, a charger, a tool kit, the user manual and the invoice.

## Saibya remote control
also: what remote does saibya use, transmitter, controller, jumper t14
Saibya uses the Jumper T14, a 2.4 GHz and 915 MHz ELRS radio controller.
It has Hall sensor gimbals, a 2.42 inch OLED display, 1 watt transmission power, EdgeTX firmware, foldable antennas and customisable switches.

## How to start Saibya
also: how do I turn it on, startup procedure, operating steps, power on
Start the control from the panel and turn on the MCB, then turn on the remote.
Make sure the area of operation is clear of people, then turn the on/off switch and wait for the transmitter to connect, shown by the network sign beside the battery sign on the OLED screen.
Confirm the emergency stop is not triggered before driving.

## Saibya control panel
also: mcb, fuse box, emergency stop, charging port, panel switches
The panel has an on/off switch, a charging port, an MCB for main power supply, a fuse box and an emergency stop.
Each drive is protected by a 19 mm blade fuse, and fuses above 50 amp capacity must never be fitted.
Pressing emergency cuts off the motors while the rest of the system stays active.

## Saibya storage compartment
also: electronics box, where do I mount a computer, uart, power pins
Saibya's electronics storage compartment houses computers or electronics modules for upper payload attachments.
It provides a UART communication port along with 12 volt and 24 volt power pins for onboard processing, sensors or control systems.
The 24 volt and 12 volt outputs are limited to 10 amps in total.

## Saibya safety rules
also: is it dangerous, safety warning, precautions, how far should I stand, bystanders
Saibya is an industrial machine, not a toy, and mishandling can cause serious injury or property damage.
Maintain line-of-sight control at all times, test the emergency stop before every mission, and keep bystanders at least 10 metres away.
Operate only in designated areas and never exceed payload, terrain or speed limits.

## Saibya maintenance
also: how do I look after it, cleaning, charging, care, storage, battery temperature
Perform pre-operation checks on battery, wheels, remote and emergency stop before each use.
Clean the robot immediately after desert or sandy operations to remove sand from motors and bearings, and monitor battery temperature in weather above 40 degrees Celsius.
Charge fully after each use in a cool, dry location and store indoors in dry conditions.

## Saibya what not to do
also: warranty void, what should I avoid, restrictions, water, rain, modifications
Never operate without line of sight, and trigger the emergency stop immediately if visual contact is lost.
Never submerge the robot in more than 20 centimetres of water or operate it in heavy rain, and never use it on icy or slippery surfaces without traction testing first.
Never ignore warning lights or unusual motor sounds, and never modify the robot without authorisation, which voids the warranty and safety certification.
